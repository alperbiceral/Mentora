Mentora

Mentora is a personalized assistant for students to help on their study sessions. It aims to solve the problems students face: Irregular sleep patterns, loss of motivation, inconsistent study habits, and emotional stress. It helps students by analyzing student personality, student's emotional situation and course schedule. It follows Pomodoro technique to make students more focused. Students in universities face problems in managing their time for studies and have a fragmented time schedule. Also, the workload of different subjects may vary. Digital planners have the advantage of recording tasks or events in a calendar. However, a digital planner may demand a lot of work from the user. It may also have a very low degree of personalization. Students may have to transform their academic constraints, including their time tables and course syllabi, into a study plan on their own. Mentora is a tool intended to help students overcome these problems. It provides a personalized tool for supporting students in their studies. It helps students plan, execute, and monitor their studies. The tool works with the help of structured academic constraints and personal signals from the user. These signals may include the user's personality and behavioral characteristics. Based on these factors, the tool creates a study plan that is feasible and flexible for the user.

Our Solutions

Planning

Mentora automatically generates weekly study schedules. The system uses information such as course workload, importance level, and enjoyment level. This information is used by the system to create a schedule for the study sessions. This ensures that there are no conflicts with other course blocks.

Execution

The Mentora system also has other tools to help students with the execution of the study sessions. This includes a timer and a Pomodoro technique. Each study session is recorded by the system. This enables students to track their study sessions.

Support and Engagement

The Mentora system also has other tools to help students with motivation and engagement. This includes personality traits and emotions. Gamification is also another feature in the Mentora system. This includes asking students a question each day. Students also have the option of having friends in the system. This enables them to collaborate and support each other.
